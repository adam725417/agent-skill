# Pricing Rules

- Gross margin = (quoted price - total cost) / quoted price.
- Minimum standard gross margin: 12%.
- Target gross margin: 18%.
- Margin below 8%: REJECT.
- Margin from 8% to below 12%: REVIEW.
- Margin at or above 12%: passes the margin rule.
- Floor price = cost / (1 - 0.12).
- Target price = cost / (1 - 0.18).
- Show up to 3 recent WON prices when available.
- Historical price is a reference, not an automatic promise.
